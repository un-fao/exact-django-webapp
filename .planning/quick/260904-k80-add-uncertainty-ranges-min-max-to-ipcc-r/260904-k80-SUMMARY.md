Six of ten metrics -- including the two structurally hardest ones (121 ranged groups derived correctly across all 7 documented CSV quirks, and 238 columns) -- match the prototype baseline exactly. The remaining deltas live entirely in the "soft" buckets (which rows land in `base_mismatch`/`unmatched`/`carrying_bound`), not in the hard-gated numbers the plan's `<verify>` block asserts (`ranged_groups==121`, `new_columns==238`, `conflict==0`, `written>=57000` -- all pass).

Investigated the largest single-table contributor to the delta, `CropNitrousEstimationDefaultFactor` (6 mismatches) and `EnergyDefaultEmissionFactor` (11 of 11 matched rows mismatch): both are genuine CSV-vs-fixture value disagreements, not join bugs --
- `CropNitrousEstimationDefaultFactor` rows 190/193/194/195: CSV carries a non-zero `rs_t`/`n_bg_t` value where the currently committed fixture stores `0` (unset/default) for that `land_use_type`, i.e. this data drop is more complete than what's currently seeded.
- `EnergyDefaultEmissionFactor`: CSV values (e.g. `co2=69300`) and fixture values (e.g. `co2=2.27`) are off by roughly 4 orders of magnitude -- a unit mismatch between the raw IPCC table and whatever conversion produced the currently stored factors, compounded by the model's join key only covering `fuel_type` (the CSV has no `fuel_use_type` column, though the model's `unique_together` includes it) and at least one duplicate `FuelType` display name.

Both are exactly the class of problem the base-value gate exists to catch, and both were correctly skipped rather than silently written. Being more conservative than the baseline (skipping slightly more rows) is the safe direction under D-01 -- worst case, a future data-quality follow-up looks at these specific rows; no risk of writing an uncertainty bound onto the wrong fixture row.
