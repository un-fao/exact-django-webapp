I. Purpose and scope of the assessment

This annex supports the greenhouse gas (GHG) reporting of the project’s Annual Performance Report (APR) to the Green Climate Fund (GCF). It documents the GHG accounting behind the mitigation results reported against the following indicators:

  • IRMF Core indicator 1 / PMF M1: GHG emissions reduced, avoided or removed/sequestered, in tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent (tCO2eq); and
  • IRMF Core indicator 4 / PMF M9.1: hectares of natural resource areas brought under improved low-emission and/or climate-resilient management practices.

The targets reported here should be consistent with the logframe and the Funding Proposal (FP). Annual values are based on actual implementation in the reporting year, and the previous cumulative value should match what was reported in the previous APR. The EX-ACT results workbook of this project is the source workbook supporting the reported figures.
