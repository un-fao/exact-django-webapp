Result levels

  • Reduction of current emissions: activities that lower emissions already taking place (e.g. fertilizer reduction, fire management).
  • Avoidance and substitution: activities that prevent emissions that would have occurred without the project (e.g. avoided deforestation, forest conservation).
  • Removal and sequestration: activities that take up CO2 in biomass and soils (e.g. forest restoration, agroforestry).
